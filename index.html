About Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi is one of India's most loved festivals, celebrated to honor Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the symbol of wisdom, prosperity and new beginnings.

🐘

Lord Ganesha

Lord Ganesha is worshipped as the god of wisdom, knowledge and good fortune.

🙏

Devotion

Families and communities come together to offer prayers, aarti, flowers and delicious offerings.

🌺

Tradition

Decorations, music, aarti, modaks and cultural celebrations make the festival special.

BAPPA'S FAVORITE

Modak Corner

Modak is one of the most popular traditional sweets prepared during Ganesh Chaturthi.

🌰

Ukadiche Modak

Soft steamed rice-flour dumplings filled with coconut and jaggery are a traditional Maharashtrian favorite during Ganesh Chaturthi.

Simple Ingredients

Rice flour • Coconut • Jaggery • Cardamom • Ghee

Preparation

Prepare the coconut-jaggery filling, make a soft rice-flour dough, shape the modaks and steam them until cooked.
